SMS Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sweet Flypaper of Film collects, uses, and protects your mobile information when you opt in to receive operational text messages via our cast and crew registration form hosted at tally.so/r/b5kBAL.

1. Information We Collect

When you fill out our crew and cast onboarding forms, we collect personal information you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Full Name
  • Mobile Phone Number
  • Your explicit opt-in consent to receive text messages

2. How We Use Your Information

Your mobile phone number is used strictly for internal, operational production communications. Messages are sent via our text messaging provider, Twilio. Program messages include:

  • Daily call times and set location changes
  • Production schedule adjustments and weather day updates
  • Critical, time-sensitive logistics directly related to your role on set

We do not use your mobile information for marketing, promotions, or advertisements.

3. Sharing of Information (The Golden Rule)

Strict Privacy Protection: Mobile information and opt-in data collected via our forms will not be shared, sold, rented, or leased to any third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. Your data is only shared with our service processor (Twilio) strictly to facilitate delivery of our operational updates.

4. Opt-Out and Help Controls

You maintain full control over your mobile subscriptions:

  • To Stop Messages: You can cancel the SMS service at any time by replying STOP to any text message you receive from Sweet Flypaper of Film. Once you send the SMS message "STOP", we will send you a final text confirmation. After this, you will no longer receive text updates unless you explicitly re-register.
  • To Get Assistance: You can text the keyword HELP at any time to receive service information or contact production support.

Note: Standard message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier plan.

5. Data Security

We implement technical and organizational security controls to safeguard your mobile data from unauthorized access or disclosure. Access to your contact records is strictly confined to the immediate production coordination staff of Sweet Flypaper of Film.
