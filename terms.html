1. Program Description

Our SMS program is strictly operational and administrative. Sweet Flypaper of Film sends automated text messages to registered cast and crew members to coordinate active television, film, or media production logistics. Messages include daily call times, set location changes, wardrobe updates, weather alerts, and general daily scheduling adjustments.

2. Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on active production cycles and your specific shoot schedule. You will only receive messages when actively staffed or scheduled for an upcoming production day.

Supported carriers include all major US and Canadian mobile networks; however, carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

4. How to Opt-Out (Stop)

You can cancel and opt out of the SMS service at any time. To unsubscribe, simply text STOP to any message you receive from Sweet Flypaper of Film. After texting STOP, you will receive one final message confirming your unsubscribed status. No further messages will be sent unless you submit a new registration form.

5. How to Get Help

If you experience issues with the messaging system or need programmatic assistance, text HELP to our text messaging number. Alternatively, you can contact the Sweet Flypaper of Film production office directly.
